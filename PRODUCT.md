## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are curators, gallerists, and jurors evaluating the artist's work for exhibitions, open calls, residencies, or admissions — people scanning quickly to judge the work and decide whether to follow up. A secondary audience is potential clients or collaborators for art-direction/audiovisual work, since the artist is expanding her practice in that direction. There is no defined commerce audience (see Capabilities and Constraints).

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for visual artist Estefanía Bedoya Giraldo ("Kory"), presenting her work (drawing, painting, installation, short film) so viewers can quickly grasp her practice and reach her. It also serves as a personal showcase/record of her practice, without a single defined conversion goal beyond contact.

## Positioning

The work sits between the gothic and the matérico: oil pastel, pencil, and mixed installation/audiovisual media exploring memory, nostalgia, and identity, often through food and fruit as material and symbolic subjects. The stated intent is that the viewer doesn't just look at the work, but feels observed by it — a contemplative, poetic register distinct from a decorative or purely illustrative practice.

## Operating Context

- Based in Medellín, Colombia; studying Artes Plásticas at Fundación Universitaria Bellas Artes (2024–present), with partial studies in architecture.
- Also has a professional modeling and artistic makeup background, which she treats as a performative extension of her practice.
- Works span oil pastel on watercolor paper/industrial cardboard, pencil/pen, installation, and short film.
- Maintains a separate print-oriented portfolio PDF (see `PORTAFOLIO.md`) with additional biographical and technical detail; this website is the digital-facing counterpart.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No e-commerce: no prices, no purchase flow. Site is non-commercial for now; do not add a shop, pricing, or "buy" CTAs.
- Contact happens via email and Instagram (@its_koryn), already wired in the Contact section.
- Gallery content must come from real, confirmed works (see `src/data/obras.ts`); do not invent additional pieces, series, or exhibitions.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

- Let the work carry the site: curators and jurors judge fast, so imagery and craft outrank explanatory copy.
- Honor the gothic/matérico register — dark, textural, contemplative — never default to a bright, generic "creative portfolio" look.
- Keep every claim traceable to real evidence (`obras.ts`, `PORTAFOLIO.md`); no invented shows, quotes, or credentials.
- Treat contact as the only conversion: make reaching her (email/Instagram) effortless without introducing commercial framing.
